About Us

QYLITH

Building the digital fortress for the quantum era.

Our Vision

To build the world's first quantum-secure, AI-native Layer 1 blockchain infrastructure, serving as the value layer for the digital civilization of the next decade.

Our Mission

Provide quantum-safe infrastructure before the quantum era arrives. Enable native support for AI agents. Build an open, sustainable developer community.

⚠️

Why Now?

⚛️

The Quantum Threat

Google's March 2026 paper demonstrated that a fault-tolerant quantum computer with 500,000 physical qubits could break ECDSA signatures. This means the cryptographic foundations of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and all major blockchains face existential risk between 2027 and 2030.

🤖

The AI Agent Revolution

AI agents are evolving from experimental concepts into autonomous economic actors. Yet no existing blockchain provides a native execution environment for AI agents—they merely "borrow" human tools. Qylith changes that.

🏗️

Retrofitting Won't Work

Adding quantum resistance to existing chains requires complex migrations, hard forks, and compatibility issues. AI features bolted on top remain second-class citizens. Qylith is designed correctly from day one.
