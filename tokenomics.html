Deflationary Mechanism

🔥 Base Fee Burn

50% of base transaction fees are burned, reducing supply over time. As network usage grows, more QYL is removed from circulation.

🔒 Stake Lock Rewards

Extended staking periods earn bonus rewards but lock tokens from circulation. Longer locks = more rewards + more supply reduction.

💎 Quarterly Burns

Quarterly protocol revenue (bridge fees, AI agent fees) is used to buy back and burn QYL, accelerating deflation.
